An editorial code

What a scientific film is allowed to show.

Use the experiment, when it exists.

Original figures, attributable photographs, and dated laboratory recordings take precedence over stock visuals. Every clip must be linked to a real and relevant source.

Animate an idea, not a result.

Illustrative molecular models and reconstructions are explicitly distinguished from measured structures, historical facsimiles, microscopy and clinical footage. Synthetic imagery must not masquerade as data.

Leave the evidence open.

Films retain the DOI, the publication status and the limits of each experiment. An ongoing MSc study, historical interpretation or unsuccessful trial is not retrospectively presented as a successful drug or result.
