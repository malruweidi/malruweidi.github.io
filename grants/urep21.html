QNRF undergraduate research / UREP21-050-3-010

The effects of cell mechanical property alterations on the survival of lung epithelial cells under shear stress

Experimental pulmonary bioengineering: how the mechanical properties of epithelial cells affect injury under fluid-induced stress.

21st UREP cycle · research plan dated 2017 · Research team at Qatar University · Undergraduate researcher contribution

Scientific question

Cell biomechanics and experimental respiratory injury

This project asked whether modifying the mechanics of epithelial cells changes their susceptibility to stress caused by moving air–liquid interfaces. The question is pertinent to alveolar injury during airway reopening and mechanical ventilation.

The archived UREP plan names Mahmoud Abdelrasool among the participating undergraduate researchers. Subsequent peer-reviewed studies developed the experimental flow-chamber methodology and investigated pharmacological modulation of epithelial injury, providing a traceable scientific continuation of this work.

PROJECT SCOPE / ATTRIBUTION

Student research role within a supervised Qatar University team. The original QNRF research plan provides the project scope, with subsequent outputs identified separately.

Methods and research design

  • Lung epithelial cell culture and mechanical perturbation
  • Parallel-plate flow chamber and flow-induced stress
  • Live/dead fluorescence assays and morphometric analysis
  • Quantitative interpretation of cellular injury and protective treatments

Research record

Related published methods paper: Shurbaji et al., Methods and Protocols (2020), 3, 65. Related first-author experimental paper: Al-Ruweidi et al., Journal of Applied Physiology (2021).
