The scientific and personal record

Evidence has a history.
So do scientists.

The archive reaches back to the school chemistry exhibition I organized in 2009, before the 2011 anatomy lesson, and continues through laboratory benches, chemistry festivals, international meetings and research visits. Its photographs show the people, instruments and scientific communities around the work, including the ordinary days that never became a paper. Dates are shown where the surviving records support them, and the research and community activities remain documented in their original context.
