## Figure and media release boundaries

The research profile uses **original publication links**, with Figure 3 explicitly identified for the pulmonary result, and source studies for the PLA peel result and zebrafish recordings. It **does not copy publisher-owned microscopy images, charts or clinical data into a new website asset without separate figure attribution and reuse review**. A suitable future figure-release procedure is: locate original panel; establish license or permission; verify axis, units and control groups; add exact citation and panel ID; label any recreated graphic as a reconstruction; retain the full uncropped source.
