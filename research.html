Research investigations · 11 years, 2015–2026

What the experiments actually asked.

My first research project in 2015 examined the optical response of curcumin to cyanide. Later work took me into zebrafish vascular imaging, polymer surface modification, mechanically injured lung cells and inhalable drug carriers. I had to change methods each time, but one question grew more demanding: which observation would actually test the explanation? The investigations below include experiments that succeeded, procedures that failed and the work I carried out with collaborators.
