Making Medicines Keep.

Why a medieval Cairene pharmacy needed recipes that were dependable beyond the moment of preparation. The new edition is built around a genuine manuscript witness to the pharmacy handbook, with separate illustrations of syrup making, crystallization and substitution.

A film about pharmaceutical reliability in medieval Cairo, based on an accepted historical research article and surviving pharmacy manuscripts.
