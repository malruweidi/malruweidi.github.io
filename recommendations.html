01 / ESSAY
Scientific integrity / essay

Cargo Cult Science

Richard P. Feynman · 1974

Feynman’s Caltech commencement address is a compact lesson in the difference between the appearance of research and the discipline of honest inquiry. The decisive test is whether an investigator discloses the evidence, uncertainties and experimental defects that might weaken their preferred explanation.

Read at Caltech ↗Original publication record ↗
Why it stays on the shelf

For any scientific project, ask what result would change your mind and whether you have made it possible for that result to appear.

First published in Engineering and Science, 37(7), pp. 10–13.
