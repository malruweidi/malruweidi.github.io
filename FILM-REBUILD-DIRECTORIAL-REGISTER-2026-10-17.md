## New visual language: five distinct directing modes

**Witness.** First-person personal archives, failures, illness and microscope. Patient shots, accurate dates, photographic sequence and meaningful silent intervals. Do not supply fake footage of the owner's experiments.

**Mechanism.** Scientific papers and methods. Uncropped primary figure first, then a precise diagram/animation, back to actual measurements, explain inferential limit. Do not reuse a generic background as a substitute for the study's data.

**Document.** History and textual scholarship. Archival manuscript image (licensed), original text in a scholarly typographic treatment, historical handling of materials, marked comparative artefact and a final source claim. No pseudo-Arabic, forged folio, imagined manuscript page or bogus Cairo photograph.

**Material.** Polymer and formulation. Real physical object/texture, the parameter it tests, controlled animated transformation, actual spectrum/micrograph, and the material's failure mode. A CAD-style beauty shot is not evidence.

**Argument.** Essays and columns. Evidence on screen (source quotation/page/screenshot with rights), crisp original diagrams, counterexample, consequence. These are essays in film form, not motivational commercials.

### Scientific papers, methods and thesis

- **airway-reopening:** Open on a moving meniscus contacting an epithelial layer; show original flow-chamber figure and actual measured readouts; test what dexamethasone and transdehydroandrosterone alter; end at the in-vitro/clinical boundary.
- **cerebral-cilia:** Start with real moving developing zebrafish vasculature rather than a generic cell icon; isolate endothelial cilia, then the difficulty of relating position to hemodynamic context. Source actual recorded microscopy when attributable.
- **curcumin-cyanide:** Begin with real before/after fluorescence or absorbance from the paper; build the probe response with the actual molecular structure and optically distinguish solution detection from living-cell experiments.
- **echo-cnn-lstm:** Show a fetal echocardiography sequence only if rights, data privacy, and publication provenance are verified; animate the temporal information entering the CNN/LSTM model; emphasize pilot design, validation, and limitations.
- **pla-grafting:** Start with a visually identical pair of PLA surfaces; move into real plasma-assisted chemistry and original spectroscopy/surface characterization; do not confuse bulk PLA with its grafted layer.
- **pla-scaffolds:** Macro shot of scaffold fibers, surface modification, antimicrobial readout as reported, test conditions and controls. Do not show generic microbes being “destroyed” as invented results.
- **flow-chamber-method:** Build the chamber in an accurate exploded animated section; reassemble around the controlled interface; actual geometry and readouts; mark illustrative dimensions as such.
- **zebrafish-hemodynamics:** True high-speed recording, play slow against the heartbeat, explain frame extraction and measurement, then show the original study's quantitative result. Do not use AI simulation as though it were real circulation.
- **ace2-covid-drugs:** Separate treatment groups and measured soluble ACE2/angiotensin II from theoretical receptor signaling; use figure-safe animation and explain association versus causation.
- **niosomes:** Begin at the actual nebulizer challenge; sequence formulation preparation, DLS, vesicle integrity, aerosol deposition and uncertainty. Ongoing master's thesis, no made-up values or clinical success.
- **slit2-robo:** Begin with one receptor signaling in two tumor contexts; branch the mechanistic pathways; use the patent review's actual scope and distinguish intellectual-property claims from efficacy.
- **ace2-forms:** Physically separate membrane-bound and soluble ACE2 in a molecular-scale visual; then examine tissue context and measurement limitations instead of a “COVID virus animation.”
- **microfluidic-virus:** Let a droplet enter a channel in a geometrically grounded animation; show capture/detection steps and analytical performance requirements from the reviewed literature.
- **drug-release-twins:** Align an experimental release curve with a model prediction; reveal where the model's residuals appear. A model is useful because it can be wrong and tested.
- **heart-failure-sensors:** Compare sensor signal against a clinical interpretation; no fictional patient dashboards or asserted predictive performance.

- **food-printing:** Watch a print line deform as rheology fails; compare yield stress and shape retention with real source illustrations; accepted chapter, not a completed experiment.
- **ceramics-printing:** Build a bio-ceramic scaffold through processing and defect formation, then ask what material tests precede biocompatibility claims; no fictional implantation trial.
- **cubosome-theranostics:** Show the actual cubic-phase topology with labeled simulated geometry; compare loading/release logic, then translational constraints; no fake TEM image.
- **micelles-pahs:** Connect polymer amphiphilicity and PAH partitioning to authentic unsuccessful polymer trials; never turn a grant proposal or failed dispersion into a positive result.

## Asset sourcing and rights

Priority order: (1) owner's original authenticated photos/videos; (2) figures from the user's papers with publication-rights check; (3) public-domain museum or institutional images with item identifiers; (4) licensed documentary stock relevant to the actual mechanism; (5) clearly marked original visualizations and cinematic reconstructions. Generate original art only where the sequence is better communicated by reconstruction. Do not use synthetic video to impersonate a laboratory outcome.

Metropolitan Museum of Art historical image catalogue (public domain, verified 17 October 2026):
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/451400 — *Preparing Medicine from Honey*, Abdullah ibn al-Fadl, Baghdad, 621 AH/1224 CE.
- https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/446288 — *Physician Preparing an Elixir*, same attribution/year.

Use the Met API object ID and public-domain flag before any automated download, preserve the object's metadata in the video source manifest. A museum's public-domain folio may be visual evidence of **art and the broader historical practice**, not evidence that the author's Cairo prescription looked like it.

Third-party internet photographs require a rights ledger with creator, source URL, image date, specific license and license retrieval date. A Google Images result is not a reuse license. Where published article figures cannot be republished, link to the source and produce a fact-checked explanatory diagram.

## Technical/creative release gate

1. **Scientific/historical editor:** every sentence and visual label traceable to the primary source; qualification correctly preserved.
2. **Visual editor:** original visual sources identified, no synthetic “evidence,” sufficient meaningful changes of image, varied pacing, no repetitive templates, no unnecessary slow zooms.
3. **Audio editor:** three or more blind-comparison narrator auditions, Arabic/Latin/scientific term checks by a fluent reader, controlled score under dialogue; mechanical loudness alone is not approval.
4. **Viewing:** full uninterrupted desktop and iPhone screening with subtitles; charts readable when paused; no awkward frame cropping; subtitles synchronized to the actual final voice.
5. **Release engineering:** exact MP4 and VTT hashes, verified opening metadata, license/source credits, an archived previous export, reversible player switch; performance budget and HTTP video loading checked on actual site.

Current branch contains two-minute treatments of the three pilots in assets/films/directors-cuts-v2.json, and a separate experimental renderer. Preview files, if built, are **not** final, published, or human-auditioned. Do not silently promote them because automated checks pass.

## Tool evaluation

- FFmpeg: deterministic assembly, filters, audio loudness analysis and encoding; use as the reproducible foundation.
- Kdenlive (GPL open source): useful for actual editorial review of pacing, J/L cuts and keyframing when visual hand-finish is warranted.
- Remotion: highly suitable for reproducible scientific diagrams; check the license against organization size before commercial deployment.
- Blender: scientific 3D mechanisms when geometrical meaning justifies 3D.
- Chatterbox: audition for expressive local TTS; expensive relative to Kokoro in runtime/VRAM; no unauthorized voice cloning.
- Qwen3-TTS: audition for voices/phonetic accuracy when suitable GPU available, subject to actual human evaluation.
- Kokoro: lightweight fallback for assembly drafts, **not** automatically selected as final voice merely because the renderer uses it today.
- Wan/ComfyUI: optional short conceptual atmospheric inserts with explicit disclosure, never fabricated experiment, patient, or period historical evidence.

Public user studies/community input: https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/comments/1tzv1uz/ and https://www.reddit.com/r/documentaryfilmmaking/comments/1wwr1g6/ — these supply subjective workflows, not benchmark results. The key editorial point: stillness is an intentional option; motion should help the argument rather than advertise an effect.
