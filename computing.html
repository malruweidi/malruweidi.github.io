01 / Published artificial intelligence research

Fetal echocardiography.
Deep learning, clinically grounded.

In a 2023 IEEE Access pilot study, I shared first-authorship credit for work on detecting congenital heart disease from fetal echocardiograms using stacked CNN–LSTM models.

Peer-reviewed / Equal first contribution

From ultrasound sequences to interpretable evidence.

Rahman T, Al-Ruweidi MKAA, et al. IEEE Access (2023), 11:110375–110390. DOI: 10.1109/ACCESS.2023.3316719

Read original publication ↗

The study investigated a computer-vision approach to fetal congenital heart disease screening, with particular attention to hypoplastic left heart syndrome. The original paper reported a pilot cohort of 9 healthy and 13 HLHS cases; its modelling workflow compared MobileNetV2, ResNet18, ResNet50, DenseNet121 and GoogLeNet CNN–LSTM architectures and used a stacked model with cross-validation. This sample size limits generalisability, and the reported metrics must not be mistaken for external clinical validation.

My documented contribution includes ultrasound data curation and preprocessing, model evaluation, clinical interpretation, figures and manuscript development. Equal first authorship is explicitly recorded in the revised academic CV. The study was a pilot, not a claim of clinically deployed or prospectively validated diagnostic software.

02 / Open-source scientific software

Drug delivery design.
Transparent by construction.

NanoFormulationDesigner is a materials-aware preformulation decision-support workbench. It encodes physical-chemistry assumptions and curated properties rather than presenting unvalidated AI output as experimental fact.

Open-source repository / Working software

NanoFormulationDesigner

Python package, Streamlit interface and command-line tools for screening nanocarrier compositions, route constraints, descriptor-supported formulation tendencies and laboratory design choices. The repository includes tests, data provenance documentation and a material-property database.

Pharmaceutical modelling / Thesis-linked development

Formulation physics and descriptor calculations

Scientific Python workflows for HLB mixtures, critical packing parameter, Tanford chain geometry, Hansen solubility distance, Flory–Huggins interaction concepts and ionization calculations. These support experimental planning for vesicular systems, including the ongoing inhalable niosome/proniosome research.

Visit pulmonary formulation research ↗

What the software can show

Reproducible reasoning, not manufactured certainty.

The code makes calculations inspectable, warns about missing material parameters and provides explainable screening recommendations.

Molecular or material properties ↓ Physicochemical descriptors ↓ Formulation design checks ↓ Ranked tendencies + uncertainty ↓ Experimental screening plan

Repository boundary: descriptor-based decision support is not a validated predictor of encapsulation efficiency, particle size, stability, toxicity, biodistribution or clinical performance. The optional LLM text layer is not the source of physical constants.

Research philosophy / Attribution

The computation should be
as auditable as the experiment.

AI, classical modelling, statistical analysis and engineering software each answer different scientific questions. I do not use “machine learning” as a substitute for reporting a model, its dataset, its limitations and my specific contribution.
