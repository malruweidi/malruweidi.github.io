Ruwaidix / a scientific film collection

The experiment has
more than one life.

The films begin with experiments and objects from my research life: a moving air–liquid interface that injured cultured lung cells, an embedding preparation that failed at the microtome, and a microscope I brought home from Cairo. Each documentary is tied to the photographs, figures and research records behind its account.

تعود الأفلام إلى الصور والتسجيلات والأبحاث الأصلية: إصابة خلايا الرئة عند إعادة فتح المجرى الهوائي، ومحاولة إعداد المقاطع النسيجية التي فشلت، والمجهر الذي حملته من القاهرة.

Released · 03:00 scientific evidence cut

What Happens When an Airway Reopens?

One bubble, cultured lung cells and the actual published measurements: 54.7% cell death without pretreatment, versus 15.1% after dexamethasone or 16.1% after transdehydroandrosterone. An in-vitro finding, not a patient-treatment claim.

Released · 03:00 · archival chemistry documentary

The Ring That Never Closed

The intended imidazopyridine route did not yield its target. Three characterized imines led to a cyanide-sensing investigation: a yellow-to-orange response, UV–visible changes near 380 and 450 nm, and difficult questions left by exploratory cell-line work.
