The experiment has
more than one life.

There is the question imagined before the work begins, the material that eventually appears on the bench, and the evidence that survives. These films and original recordings follow that distance. When an experiment did not work, the record stays that way.

04 / editorial standard

How to read the moving record.

Moving images have evidentiary limits. The film and the experiment are not interchangeable, and a new reconstruction does not become original footage merely because it looks convincing.

Observed

Actual photographs and recordings carry the original context, with capture dates and identifiers qualified when uncertain.

Interpreted

Figures, captions, narration and diagrams explain what the measurements may support, with links to the scientific or archival source.

Proposed

Future films and illustrated treatments are labeled as unproduced. Hypotheses remain hypotheses, and unfinished work remains unfinished.
