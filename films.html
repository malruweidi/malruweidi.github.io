Ruwaidix / films and their sources

The film begins where
the figure ends.

A synthesis route ended with a different compound. A tissue block broke apart before it could yield a useful section. A moving air–liquid interface injured cultured lung cells. Each film begins with a turning point—but its scientific conclusion must come from the underlying record.

تبدأ الحكاية أحيانًا حيث تنتهي الصورة العلمية المنشورة. في أحد التجارب أصابت واجهة متحركة بين الهواء والسائل خلايا رئوية مزروعة. وفي أخرى تعثّر تحضير مقطع نسيجي قابل للفحص. وبين هذه المشاهد مجهر أحضرته من القاهرة وأسئلة لم تنتهِ بانتهاء التجربة.

Released · 03:00 · archival chemistry documentary

The Ring That Never Closed

The intended imidazopyridine route did not yield its target. Three characterized imines led to a cyanide-sensing investigation: a yellow-to-orange response, UV–visible changes near 380 and 450 nm, and difficult questions left by exploratory cell-line work.

Released · 02:00 · laboratory documentary

Two Materials. No Section.

I tried to embed zebrafish specimens for histological sectioning, but the agarose and paraffin separated during processing. The attempt yielded no interpretable tissue section. Three photographs from the preparation anchor the film.

Released · 03:00 · pulmonary research

What Happens When an Airway Reopens?

One bubble, cultured lung cells and the actual published measurements: 54.7% cell death without pretreatment, versus 15.1% after dexamethasone or 16.1% after transdehydroandrosterone. An in-vitro finding, not a patient-treatment claim.
