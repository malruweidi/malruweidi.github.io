Ruwaidix / a scientific film collection

The experiment has
more than one life.

The films begin with experiments and objects from my research life: a moving air–liquid interface that injured cultured lung cells, an embedding preparation that failed at the microtome, and a microscope I brought home from Cairo. Each documentary is tied to the photographs, figures and research records behind its account.

04 / editorial standard

How to read the moving record.

Each film distinguishes original footage and research figures from explanatory reconstructions. The study methods and source material are linked alongside the scenes.

Explore the scientific evidence reader ↗

Observed

Actual photographs and recordings carry the original context, with capture dates and identifiers qualified when uncertain.

Interpreted

Figures, captions, narration and diagrams explain what the measurements may support, with links to the scientific or archival source.

Proposed

Future films and illustrated treatments are labeled as unproduced. Hypotheses remain hypotheses, and unfinished work remains unfinished.
