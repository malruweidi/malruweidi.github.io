These records belong beside my experimental and professional work, not in place of it. In particular, the CITI modules on individual animal species document coursework. They should not be mistaken for evidence that I have personally handled every species or received authorization for a particular animal procedure.

For CITI Program records, the university named is the institution whose training requirements governed completion, not the issuer of a university degree or a claim of employment or research affiliation. Udemy certificates are certificates of course completion; words such as “accredited” in a course title are reproduced only as part of the instructor's published title.
