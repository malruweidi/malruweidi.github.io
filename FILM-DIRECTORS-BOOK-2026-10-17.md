# RUWAIDIX — FILM DIRECTOR'S BOOK
17 October 2026 • Working production brief • Never automatically replaces a live film

## Diagnosis from the actual repository
35 publication/essay films use a common six-scene, 84-second model. This is an automated format, not 35 independently directed screenplays. Some visual files recur across unrelated research subjects; static template overlay, identical score style and a single narrator strengthen the impression of sameness. The existing eight long-form films need their own critical review as well.

## Editorial rules
Begin with an image, physical contradiction, instrument or observed result, not with credentials or 'I have always been interested in'. Every scene should show the particular claim while it is heard. A scientific figure stays legible; the editor never changes axes, erases controls or invents data. Separate the following on-screen: personal archive / original laboratory evidence / published figure / third-party context photograph / historically comparative illustration / AI-generated editorial reconstruction. Stock imagery must never substitute for the author's own experiment. Context photos of Cairo are modern unless captioned otherwise. No invented 13th-century prescription facsimile.

Speech: narration is an argument and an experience, not a bibliography. Write in complete natural sentences, vary sentence length, and read the whole script aloud. Prohibit filler ('journey', 'passion', 'intersection', 'revolutionize'), generic cliffhangers, moralizing conclusions, and duplicate dialogue between screen text and voice. Films may vary from 60 to 180 seconds according to the story, never because a generator forces six equal acts. Human voices or authorized recordings are preferred. For synthetic narration, audition separate masters blind and verify every scientific proper noun. Never clone an identifiable person's voice without consent.

Picture: use contact sheets to select only the strongest personal photographs, native science video and source figures. Search Wikimedia Commons, Wellcome, NIH, museum open-access collections, and trusted institutional collections case by case, logging URL, rights, creator, date, scene purpose, and whether cropped. NIH/NIGMS gallery material is generally CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, so do not presume unrestricted commercial rights. AI illustrations are possible for unobservable mechanisms but must be identified, never claimed as experiments. The approved website design and its complete original archive are preserved.

Sound and editing: choose music because it supports the actual dramatic stakes, not because a universal synth loop is available. Preserve silence when needed; no unearned epic swell. For each candidate master inspect waveform, integrated loudness, true peak, plosives, multilingual terms, subtitle synchronization and playback on a narrow phone. Crossfades cannot conceal bad pacing. Test hard cuts, match cuts and actual documentary movement.

## Unique film treatments (35 / 35)

### 01 — airway-reopening
Cold open on the advancing air-liquid boundary, not a respiratory stock video. Animate chamber geometry beside actual paper figures, then show the injury assay and pharmacological perturbation; finish at the culture-to-patient boundary.

### 02 — cerebral-cilia
Start with a real frame of a living cranial vascular network and follow one vessel. Use the archived zebrafish moving microscopy, tracked microanatomy and cilia distribution figures; describe observed distributions without claiming an unmeasured sensing mechanism.

### 03 — slit2-robo
Begin with the same signalling axis producing different outcomes in two cancer contexts. Animate the ligand/receptor interface, split the opposing biological contexts and annotate the patent landscape; no generic tumour footage or sweeping benefit claim.

### 04 — curcumin-cyanide
Open on a chromatic or fluorescent signal changing after analyte exposure. Show the synthesis, photophysical response and cell-based result from actual figures; distinguish a sensing assay from a clinical cyanide detector.

### 05 — making-medicines-keep
A preparation leaves the heat. What happens by tomorrow? Investigate julab, qiwam, crystallization, a Cairo Genizah prescription and substitution using source-attributed museum images and editorial process artwork; never pass Baghdad manuscript art off as the Cairo text.

### 06 — niosomes
A perfect-looking carrier approaches a nebulizer, where its first practical challenge begins. Film actual materials and bench apparatus where provenance is known, animate Span 60/cholesterol bilayers and particle trajectories, include DLS/FTIR/impactor methods; never invent thesis results.

### 07 — echo-cnn-lstm
Begin on the movement within an echocardiography cine-loop rather than a heart icon. Use rights-cleared real cine-loop or openly licensed educational cardiology footage, diagram CNN feature extraction and LSTM sequence modelling, and show reported evaluation with cohort limitations.

### 08 — pla-grafting
A surface looks unchanged to the eye even as its chemistry changes. Use actual PLA film photographs, simple plasma line animation, reported characterization and surface responses; do not imply surface grafting has transformed bulk properties.

### 09 — pla-scaffolds
Start with the actual fibre/scaffold morphology before any conceptual rendering. Intercut real microscopy, treatment procedure and antimicrobial assay figures, explaining which measured property changes; avoid generic medical implant footage.

### 10 — flow-chamber-method
Show a blank chamber filling; a moving meniscus becomes the plot. Build a mechanically accurate schematic from the published methods, compare controlled variables and readout types, preserve cell/organ limitations.

### 11 — zebrafish-hemodynamics
The first image is a genuine heartbeat that the eye cannot resolve. Use original high-speed microscopy and timed frame-by-frame analysis; annotate optical path, measurement and uncertainty rather than a decorative fish animation.

### 12 — ace2-covid-drugs
Open on the medication and infection interpretation problem, not a generic coronavirus. Distinguish soluble and membrane ACE2 and the study's measured association; contextualize hypertension, confounding and noncausal inference.

### 13 — gi-autoimmunity-1
Move from actual intestinal microanatomy to the immune barrier rather than patient stock footage. Use rights-cleared micrographs, immune signalling diagrams, and mechanisms explicitly discussed in the chapter; avoid depicting one disorder as a universal explanation.

### 14 — gi-autoimmunity-2
Start with apparently similar symptoms leading to different diagnostic pathways. Use labelled pathology imagery, diagnostic pathway branching and the chapter's historical therapeutic landscape; signal that 2022 literature is not current clinical guidance.

### 15 — heart-failure-sensors
Open on a real waveform struggling with noise, not a beating-heart icon. Use real annotated ECG/echo/sensor traces, signal processing and data latency; distinguish monitoring/forecasting from validated clinical prediction.

### 16 — ace2-forms
A molecule exists at the membrane; a soluble version enters circulation. Animate two compartments and distinct biological consequences, keeping review-based hypotheses separate from measured findings.

### 17 — microfluidic-virus
Watch a tiny droplet enter the channel; everything depends on the fluid path. Show real microfluidic chip photos where rights allow, collection, preparation, assay readouts, limits of specificity and matrix effects.

### 18 — drug-release-twins
Put a measured release curve beside a model that initially fails. Animate differential equations and changing parameters, then compare model predictions with independent experimental data; no fabricated validation graph.

### 19 — food-printing
A printed shape collapses: the nozzle was accurate, the material was not. Use public license printing footage with accurate labels, shear thinning, recovery and shape retention concept art; accepted chapter is a critical review, not a new experiment.

### 20 — ceramics-printing
An open lattice promises a scaffold until fabrication defects arrive. Show microscope-safe images of ceramic microstructure, layer-by-layer manufacturing, porosity–strength conflict and biological translation; no falsely implied patient implant.

### 21 — cubosome-theranostics
The apparently elegant internal cubic geometry creates new delivery problems. Build accurate schematic bilayer topology, microscopy examples only when licensed, and translation gates such as biodistribution/scale-up; forthcoming chapter, not proven therapy.

### 22 — micelles-pahs
Water refuses a hydrophobic pollutant; the formulation must persuade it. Use the actual polymer trial archive, phase behaviour diagram and Flory–Huggins conceptual analysis; retain failed or inconclusive attempts.

### 23 — heterocycle-cancer
The molecule drawn on paper confronts a reaction mixture and its spectra. Use owner-supplied synthesis board/bench media, reaction scheme and spectra with controls; never infer anticancer effectiveness from structure alone.

### 24 — histology-failure
The first 3 seconds are the failed microtome ribbon, not the intact embryo. Intercut the true agarose/paraffin photographs with phase-interface material diagrams, distinguish plausible mechanism from measured cause.

### 25 — cairo-microscope
Start at home with the personally purchased instrument, not an expensive university microscope. Use real 2017 desk imagery, Cairo/Kasr El-Einy contextual footage and the travel of an instrument from Cairo to Qatar; connect to unscheduled observation.

### 26 — essay-life-between-diagnoses
Keep the person's voice and real archival material; do not aestheticize suffering. Use dated personal photos and excerpts with privacy controls, the acute attack before US travel and lost medical-school opportunity accurately; no fictional hospital performance.

### 27 — essay-ai-email
Open on an ordinary email that becomes a misplaced interrogation of authorship. Use typography, email-interface animation and contrast of authority, language and class; do not use fake screenshots of actual individuals.

### 28 — essay-machine-language
Begin with an older institutional memo sounding suspiciously synthetic. Use historical examples and self-critical line editing on screen; push the argument, not generic AI robot imagery.

### 29 — essay-academic-mental-health
Begin with the omissions in an academic CV rather than a sad researcher stock shot. Use archival notebooks, institutional deadlines and essay text; avoid diagnostic insinuation about colleagues.

### 30 — essay-scientific-credit
The award photograph is the visible end of an invisible research network. Use licensed archival discovery photographs and diagrams of credited collaborations, primary source dates and careful historiography.

### 31 — essay-tf-states
A beautifully defined pocket disappears when the full regulatory complex moves. Use PDB structures with source IDs, conformational animation and target engagement controls; keep states distinguishable from static crystal structures.

### 32 — essay-potency-exposure
Show the cherished nanomolar assay result, then ask what concentration reaches tissue. Animate free drug concentration–time and target occupancy with clearly labelled hypothetical curves; tie to measured PK/PD literature.

### 33 — essay-trna
Begin at the ribosome with the adaptor arriving at a codon. Use accurate molecular animation and tRNA structural references, highlight the actual intervention and liability mechanisms.

### 34 — essay-proximity
The drug binds two proteins and makes an event happen. Use a readable molecular proximity animation, catalytic degradation or stabilization pathways as appropriate, and differentiate event-driven from occupancy-driven pharmacology.

### 35 — essay-adc
The antibody binds the cell; the real challenge begins after binding. Animate internalization, linker cleavage, payload distribution and off-target exposure; show actual reported structures/figures with attribution.

## Long-form films already in the collection

**What Survives the Experiment:** Recut around a material constraint, not twelve résumé chapters. The 2011 anatomy lesson, owned Cairo microscope, real zebrafish recordings, failed embedding material, flow chamber and inhalable formulation can form a narrative about changing experimental standards. Preserve the claim boundaries and avoid chronology-as-list.

**What a Bubble Can Do / Airway Evidence Cut:** Prefer moving interface models, actual authored paper figures and measured cellular readouts. Do not reuse the same chamber schematic to fill eight chapters.

**Two Materials. No Section.:** Close photographs of the failed agarose/paraffin section, sound of the instrument only if authentic and permitted, an explanatory materials animation. This is a story of failure, not a post hoc validated mechanism.

**The Laboratory We Carried With Us:** Source each Chem Squad/ACS photograph specifically, use original group recordings, avoid claims about an archival scene that its provenance cannot support.

**The Microscope I Brought from Cairo:** Let authentic home microscope photographs, instrument details and geography carry the narration. No embellishment of transport or ownership.

**The Ring That Never Closed:** Open on actual research artifact and mechanism; respect original unsuccessful experimental data and boundaries.

**Making Medicines Keep:** Pilot director's cut: eight chapter questions, sixteen individually credited picture shots, licensed contextual historic materials, quiet and specific narration. A branch preview, not a live replacement until playback review.

## Production stack and licensing decisions

Core: FFmpeg + Python/Pillow/OpenCV for reproducible cut assembly and source containment; Kdenlive/MLT or Blender for human finishing, masking and scientific motion graphics. Open TTS candidates include Apache-2.0 Qwen3-TTS, MIT Chatterbox and lightweight Kokoro; choice requires pronunciation/audio audition, compute testing and model-weight-license check. Reddit's October 2026 video-editing discussion favors DaVinci Resolve overall and Kdenlive/Olive among open-source editors. Remotion is excellent for React-based compositions but is **source-available under a proprietary license**, not OSI open-source: do not describe it as a fully open alternative.

## Release gates

1. Fact review against original publication and each manuscript status. 2. Visual provenance/rights checked by shot. 3. Voice pronunciation and naturalness listened to in full. 4. Silent cut comprehensible, all figures legible on mobile. 5. Captions timed, no fabricated evidence, suitable volume. 6. A 120-second video exists, is playable, carries hash, and has explicit provenance metadata. 7. Swap the website's primary MP4 only after passing creative and scientific review; keep old version linked in archive.
