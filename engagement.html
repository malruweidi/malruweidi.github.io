Research / Leadership / Public engagement

Science beyond the laboratory.

My scientific work has included building student research communities, communicating chemistry, reviewing school research and developing experimental imaging practice. These records distinguish documented activities from the scientific outputs that followed them.

2015–2019 · Chapter leadership

Helping build a chemistry community at Qatar University.

As a founder and leader of the American Chemical Society student chapter at Qatar University, I helped develop a student-led setting for workshops, chemistry demonstrations, scientific discussion and engagement with the wider community. The chapter's work emphasized opportunities for young chemists to acquire technical skills and communicate chemistry outside the classroom. Among the documented activities were Chem Talks, hands-on instrumental-methods training for 29 students, and chemistry demonstrations.

One contemporaneous record is the August 2018 poster at the 256th ACS National Meeting & Exposition, titled “ACS Qatar University's Chapter: Updates on the Approach of Qatar University's Chapter to Empower Young Chemists and Reach Out to the Community.” Its scope documents chapter development, academic growth, community outreach, public service and professional development. The poster is a historical record of the collective chapter, not evidence that every event was personally delivered by one individual.

Evidence: The LinkedIn attachment carries the label “Chapter Presentation – 2019,” while the scholarly record dates the poster to August 2018. See the poster record (DOI: 10.13140/RG.2.2.32073.67684) and the original LinkedIn media; the upload label should not be treated as the date of first presentation.

Scientific evaluation · Qatar

Evaluating student research at Al Thakhira Youth Center.

I was invited by Qatar's Ministry of Education and Higher Education and Al Thakhira Youth Center to serve as a judge for research projects presented by students from schools in northern Qatar. The invitation extended my scientific engagement into student research evaluation, where experimental logic, evidence and clear communication matter as much as the final presentation.

This is a research judging activity, not a research grant, teaching appointment or scientific publication. The photograph below shows the center associated with the event; it was cropped from the LinkedIn screenshot provided for this account and is not an original-resolution event photograph.

Public science communication

Speaking about chemistry where the public can participate.

My chemistry outreach included speaking at a Chemistry Festival at Qatar University, alongside the chapter's science-communication and demonstration activities. This work belongs to the same scientific philosophy as my later research mentoring: experimental science should be explained, questioned and made accessible without diluting its rigor.

The relevant festival photographs have been reported as present on LinkedIn; the precise post and festival edition still require source-level confirmation before photographs or a year are attributed here.

Experimental biology · Microscopy

Watching development at the scale of the whole organism.

My zebrafish work involved handling and observing embryos and larvae, microscopy, developmental assessment and imaging workflows relevant to vascular biology and experimental physiology. The images supplied with this record depict distinct developmental appearances, including chorion-enclosed embryos and a hatched larva. They are examples of biological material observed under the microscope, not a quantified phenotypic result.

Reliable scientific interpretation requires the original metadata: magnification, embryo age in hours post-fertilization, genotype, imaging modality, specimen identity and experimental condition. These details cannot be inferred from photographs alone, and are deliberately not invented here.
