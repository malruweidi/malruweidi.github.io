ESSAYS / PUBLIC SCIENTIFIC WRITING

Writing that lives outside the journal.

I have been taught to write passively, watched people cite papers that do not support their claims, and seen how easily scientific recognition can become detached from the work itself. Outside the journal, I can write about those experiences in my own voice. I also write about illness, academic life and what institutions can overlook while everyone is busy measuring success.
