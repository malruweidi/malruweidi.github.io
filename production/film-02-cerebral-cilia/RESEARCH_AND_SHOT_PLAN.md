## The real story

The study located primary cilia on endothelial cells in developing cranial vessels, particularly at nascent vascular spaces and growing hindbrain vascular branches. It reported time-dependent cilium conformation changes before the onset of blood flow. Disruption of cardiac contractility did not erase their early spatial distribution; interruption of ciliary biogenesis was associated with intracerebral hemorrhage. High shear stress was associated with premature cilia disassembly and hemorrhage.

**Critical scientific safeguards:** This is embryonic zebrafish work; neither the visuals nor narration should imply human clinical outcomes. Do not invent fluorescence intensities, flow velocities, quantified hemorrhage rates, or measurements not reported in the paper. Distinguish the observation of pre-flow ciliary dynamics from a general claim that all endothelial primary cilia are actively beating propellers.
