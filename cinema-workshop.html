A picture-led editorial investigation / October 2026

Less slideshow.
More cinema.

We examined the actual exported frames of every documentary in the Ruwaidix collection. The question is not whether the video plays, but whether the photographs, research and the argument deserve the way they have been filmed.

The contact sheets below contain six frames decoded from each published film, not mock-ups. Their supporting captions identify the problem and the director's proposed remedy. Pixel similarity and image counts are triage signals; they cannot judge the emotional truth of a film or how natural a voice sounds. No original film, Story, scientific figure or archive has been replaced by this audit.

How we rebuild

A director's cut has to earn its place.

Before another automated batch, three very different films are being re-conceived around the primary material that gives each story its stakes. The current movies remain available until a new short pilot proves its directing, narration, pictorial accuracy, and mobile readability.

Pilot 01 / Scientific failure

Two Materials. No Section.

Open on the real failed microtome block. Make the physical breakdown intelligible before showing a diagram. The absence of usable sections becomes the ending.

Pilot 02 / Historical pharmacy

Making Medicines Keep

Observe material change in syrup before introducing recipes, manuscripts and substitution. All modern reconstructions are clearly identified.

Pilot 03 / Scientific community

The Laboratory We Carried With Us

Make the people large enough to see. Let the real setting and friendships determine the film's rhythm, not the fixed chapter-title template.
