The 2021 final report / chemical evidence

The molecules we actually had.

The original proposal called for substituted imidazopyridines. The final report documents three Schiff-base compounds instead. Their characterization and response to anions became a substantial part of the recorded chemical work. It does not retroactively make the missing heterocycles successful.

The record behind the film

From an intermediate to a question.

Schiff bases were an intermediate in the initial plan. What the report eventually contains is experimental characterization and a documented optical response to anions. Other compounds behaved differently, so a visible colour shift did not by itself settle selectivity or practical reliability.

There was another strand to the collaborative project: early tests on breast-cancer cell lines. Their interesting results remained exploratory. Gene-expression findings did not confirm the anticipated target mechanism, and later protein-level checks were left incomplete during restricted laboratory access.

Two routes, one ambition

Our team proposed two synthetic routes toward substituted imidazopyridines, with structural characterization to come before biological evaluation. That was the plan in 2018.

PLANNED HETEROCYCLIC SYNTHESIS · 00:15–00:33

The closure did not happen

By the final report, we could not claim the intended heterocycles. The proposed ring closure had not delivered those compounds. Whatever we wrote next had to begin there.

OBSERVED PROJECT OUTCOME / 2021 · 00:33–00:53

What we could isolate

We did isolate and characterize three imines, also called Schiff bases. They had entered the project as intermediates, not the endpoint we were originally pursuing.

SCHIFF BASES / RECORDED INTERMEDIATES · 00:53–01:11

A different question

Instead of treating those compounds only as unfinished starting materials, we examined their response to anions. Cyanide produced a particularly visible change in one sensor solution.

ANION SENSING / LABORATORY REPORT · 01:11–01:28

From yellow to orange

Under the reported test conditions, a yellow solution turned orange after cyanide was added. A colour change was observable. Its meaning still had to be checked spectroscopically.

OBSERVED COLOUR RESPONSE / EXPLANATORY COLOURS · 01:28–01:46

Where the bands moved

The band near 380 nanometres declined as a new band near 450 grew. The report gave a detection limit of 1.42 times ten to the minus five molar for that sensor.

REPORTED UV–VISIBLE FEATURES / NOT A DIGITIZED SPECTRUM · 01:46–02:04

Beyond the flask

Colleagues explored two compounds, CMP5 and CMPB, in triple-negative breast-cancer cell lines. Some proliferation and migration readouts differed, but that was not evidence of a validated treatment.

EXPLORATORY COLLABORATIVE CELL WORK · 02:04–02:24

The unanswered mechanism

Some c-MYC measurements rose where we had expected the opposite, and some comparisons were not significant. Direct PRMT5 target engagement was never established.

GENE EXPRESSION / INCOMPLETE FOLLOW-UP · 02:24–02:42

What the report could say

COVID restrictions interrupted later protein-level follow-up. Our final report contained characterized imines, chemical observations, and unfinished biological questions. It did not contain the ring we had planned.

FINAL REPORT / 2021 / ACCESS RESTRICTIONS · 02:42–03:00

Study limits and production notes

What the report could establish.

The proposed imidazopyridines were not obtained through the intended route. Three imines were isolated and characterized, and a highlighted sensor showed a reported colour and UV–visible response to cyanide, with a detection limit of 1.42 × 10−5 M. The available project account does not establish reliable detection in complex environmental samples, a validated anticancer compound, or confirmed PRMT5 target engagement.

Cell proliferation, migration and gene-expression investigations were carried out collaboratively using CMP5 and CMPB. Some findings were unexpected or statistically inconclusive, and restrictions during the COVID-19 period interrupted planned protein-level experiments. The film avoids depicting unrecorded experimental success. The archival figure is contextual, and original editorial drawings are labeled as such.
