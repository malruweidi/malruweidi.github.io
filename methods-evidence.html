01 / Molecular chemistry & polymer thermodynamics

From spectra to molecular selection.

Chemical evidence begins with controlled responses and stated limits. These projects connect optical sensing, organic synthesis, and the thermodynamics of amphiphilic copolymers.

2015 project · 2019 original paper

UV–visible and fluorescence sensing

The curcumin–cyanide work used a colorimetric response and UV–visible absorbance changes near 421 and 520 nm, fluorescence quenching, selectivity testing, and cellular imaging to examine chemical response in solution and living cells.

A detectable optical response is not alone proof of molecular specificity; hydroxide interference was reported and is part of the published selectivity context.

Research role. Experimental involvement in the curcumin investigation; not the paper’s data analysis, interpretation or manuscript drafting.

The imidazopyridine project and later imine/Schiff-base sensing work required synthetic planning, compound characterization and a careful distinction between intended targets and products actually obtained.

The associated project dossier preserves unsuccessful routes rather than presenting the proposal as a completed synthesis.

QNRF UREP19 · theoretical calculations

Flory–Huggins parameters &amp; diblock-copolymer micelles

The PAH-remediation proposal considered compatibility between hydrophobic pollutants and micelle-forming polymer blocks. The Flory–Huggins interaction parameter χ offered a theoretical basis for comparing polymer–solute interactions.

A favorable theoretical estimate cannot establish measured micelle size, critical micelle concentration, actual pollutant loading, or successful remediation.

Research role. Drafted proposal material and personally performed Flory–Huggins χ-parameter calculations to support candidate diblock-copolymer selection.

02 / Biomaterials & surface engineering

A polymer’s surface is not its bulk.

The two Popelka-associated PLA studies examined whether surface activation and organic-acid modification change the behavior of films and fibrous scaffolds. Their measurements answer different questions about chemistry, topography and performance.

2020 &amp; 2021 · published materials research

Electrospinning, RF plasma &amp; grafting

The 2020 scaffold paper reports electrospun PLA fibrous mats followed by radio-frequency plasma activation and grafting of ascorbic or fumaric acid. The 2021 film study examines related plasma-assisted modification on PLA film.

Fabrication, wettability, and surface functionalization are experimentally distinct steps: changing surface energy does not automatically demonstrate antimicrobial efficacy or improved adhesion.

Research role. Experimental participation, interpretation and manuscript work with Anton Popelka and collaborators; the 2021 journal CRediT statement explicitly lists investigation and formal analysis.

Characterization in coauthored papers

FTIR–ATR, XPS, SEM, AFM &amp; contact-angle analysis

FTIR–ATR assesses functional-group changes; XPS measures near-surface elemental chemistry; SEM examines micromorphology; AFM resolves topography and nanomechanical response. Contact-angle/surface-free-energy analyses evaluate wettability.

The published PLA film article includes FTIR (Fig. 3), XPS (Fig. 4), SEM (Fig. 5), AFM (Figs. 6–7), and contact-angle measurements (Fig. 1). The scaffold paper likewise reports FTIR–ATR, SEM and AFM.

Research role. These are methods documented across coauthored publications. Individual instrument operation is not assigned to Mahmoud unless independently supported; formal investigation and analysis contributions are documented.

Biological materials performance

Antimicrobial testing &amp; adhesion measurements

The electrospun-scaffold study reports antimicrobial tests with Staphylococcus aureus and Escherichia coli. The PLA-film study additionally measures peel resistance of modified PLA/aluminum laminate interfaces.

Tests of bacterial susceptibility, surface wetting, and mechanical adhesion represent different performance endpoints and should not be conflated.

03 / Lung physiology & quantitative cell assays

Make an injury reproducible.

The airway-reopening studies used a physical perturbation, a cellular model and independent readouts of injury. The key methodological question was whether the experimental stress and its consequences could be measured consistently.

2020 published methods protocol · 2021 first-author original paper

Parallel-plate chamber &amp; bubble propagation

Confluent L2 rat pulmonary epithelial cells were cultured on collagen-coated coverslips in a parallel-plate flow chamber. Controlled air–liquid interface propagation generated a brief mechanical stress analogous to an airway-reopening event.

The 2020 protocol provides chamber assembly and operational steps. The 2021 experiment investigated drug pretreatment before bubble-induced mechanical injury.

Research role. Performed experimental cell-injury research, contributed to the protocol and led analysis, interpretation, figure preparation and writing in the 2021 first-author study.

2020 Methods and Protocols ↗2021 Journal of Applied Physiology ↗Related film ↗

Cell microscopy · quantitative analysis

Calcein-AM/ethidium staining, ImageJ &amp; phalloidin/DAPI

The studies measured live versus injured cells using calcein-AM (green) and ethidium homodimer-1 (red), then assessed fluorescence images in ImageJ. Cytoskeletal evaluation used phalloidin labeling of F-actin and DAPI nuclear counterstaining.

The protocol and original physiology paper use fluorescence imaging to separate cell viability from actin organization; statistics supported comparisons between experimental conditions.

Research role. Experimental execution, image-based analysis and scientific writing are part of the first-author pulmonary study record.

Surface biomechanics

Atomic force microscopy of treated cells

The first-author lung study used AFM as a complementary readout of epithelial-cell morphology and surface topography after drug exposure. Its Figure 5 documents representative cell morphologies.

AFM observations supplement fluorescence readouts but cannot on their own establish a molecular causal pathway for protection against injury.

Research role. Interpreted experimental results in the first-author research; instrument-specific operation should not be inferred from coauthorship alone.

Turn motion into measurement.

Zebrafish cardiovascular work crosses imaging modalities and timescales. The archive preserves the difference between experimental observations, hardware/protocol work and downstream mathematical analysis.

Fluorescence imaging, Z-stacks &amp; cilia distribution

The cerebral-vascular cilia study used live and fixed zebrafish, transgenic fluorescence, microscopy Z-series and whole-mount approaches to examine cilia-related patterns during vascular development.

Publication of these techniques establishes their use in the study, not personal execution of every imaging or histological method by each coauthor.

Research role. Experimental zebrafish/imaging work only. Mahmoud did not perform the paper’s data analysis, interpretation or manuscript writing.

2020 Micron paper · imaging hardware &amp; protocol

High-speed video microscopy, MicroZebraLab &amp; ImageJ

Brief high-speed videos of embryonic zebrafish hearts and major vessels were recorded through a microscope equipped with a high-speed camera. The study describes image-based cardiac and hemodynamic analyses using MicroZebraLab and ImageJ.

Acquisition reliability, documented camera/microscope configuration and repeatable analysis instructions are essential for converting short videos into meaningful physiological measures.

Research role. Implemented the high-speed camera/microscopy setup, performed experimental acquisition and wrote the imaging and analysis protocol and manuscript material; downstream analyses are not all claimed as personal execution.

05 / Clinical biomarkers & computational research

Clinical evidence needs its own controls.

Observational biomarker studies and diagnostic algorithms require different forms of rigor than bench experiments. Biological assays, confounding, patient classification and model validation must remain visibly distinct.

2022 Blood Pressure · hypertensive COVID-19 cohort

Soluble ACE2 &amp; angiotensin II immunoassays

The coauthored clinical investigation analyzed samples and clinical information from 200 hypertensive COVID-19 patients. The paper reports plasma ACE2 and angiotensin II measurements with ELISA-based assays, alongside disease-severity and antihypertensive-class comparisons.

A circulating ACE2 concentration is not a direct assay of tissue ACE2 expression, ACE2 enzymatic activity or therapeutic drug-target engagement.

Research role. Supported grant acquisition and research development, contributed to analysis, interpretation and manuscript writing. The available contribution record does not establish personal execution of the ELISA assays.

Primary clinical article ↗Own contribution statement ↗

Clinical research methods

Statistical comparisons, regression &amp; clinical interpretation

The ACE2 study describes log transformations where appropriate, group comparisons and linear regression using clinical biomarkers and covariates. Such analyses test association; medication-class comparisons in a non-randomized cohort do not establish drug causality.

The conceptual connection between soluble ACE2 biology, pharmacotherapy and prognosis required explicit separation of biological interpretation from statistical association.

Research role. Grant development, data analysis, interpretation and portions of manuscript writing in the ACE2 research programme.

ACE2 Materials and Methods ↗ACE2 mechanistic review ↗

2023 IEEE Access · equal first contribution

Fetal echocardiography, CNN–LSTM &amp; model stacking

The pilot study examined congenital-heart-disease classification from fetal ultrasound videos, combining convolutional feature extraction with sequence models and stacking. Reported video-level and subject-level evaluation addresses clinically important differences in the unit of prediction.

Clinical model claims depend on validation design, sample size, patient-level separation and external replication; high performance on an internal pilot dataset is not proof of generalizability.

Research role. Equal-first-author contribution to data preparation, machine-learning modeling, validation, analysis and manuscript drafting.

06 / Pharmaceutics & interdisciplinary scholarship

From formulation to defensible translation.

The doctoral-facing methods portfolio must distinguish your current inhalable-vesicle investigation from previously published work, and distinguish systematic scholarly analyses from measurements made at the bench.

MSc thesis · ongoing experimental research

Niosomes, proniosomes, DLS, FTIR &amp; nebulization

The ongoing dexamethasone-vesicle work links preparation and physicochemical characterization of niosomal/proniosomal carriers with questions about nebulization and aerosol deposition.

Hydrodynamic size and dispersity from dynamic light scattering, chemical compatibility from FTIR and aerosol behavior are complementary measurements. A particle-size result does not by itself establish a respirable dose or pharmacological exposure.

Research role. Direct ongoing MSc formulation work and characterization. No unverified numerical results, completed deposition studies or therapeutic efficacy are asserted.

2026 first-author patent review

Patent-landscape research &amp; molecular mechanism synthesis

The SLIT2–ROBO patent review examines therapeutic modulation across molecular mechanisms and intellectual-property records. Patent claims, experimental validation and clinical efficacy represent different levels of evidence.

For the ACE2 mechanistic review and other scholarly chapters, literature-based synthesis is accurately described as scholarly research rather than new experiments.

Research role. Led literature research, evidence synthesis and drafting of first-author review work. Patent review does not imply personal execution of every experimental technique reported in the underlying patents.
