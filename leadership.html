03 / Scientific leadership · 2015–2019

The ACS student chapter.

I helped establish the American Chemical Society student chapter at Qatar University in 2015, serving as president for two years and subsequently supporting its development. The chapter was a way to make chemistry accessible beyond lectures: student-led events, public demonstrations, scientific talks and chances to work with instruments.

Among the documented activities were central-laboratory instrumentation training arranged for 29 students, Chem Talks and scientific events. The chapter's work and community-outreach approach were also described in a presentation at the 2018 ACS national meeting. These were collective achievements of the chapter and its members.

04 / Professional practice · Quality systems

Quality & process management.

Organizing a chapter means keeping programmes, people and resources working together. Quality management gave me more formal methods for a related problem: how to make a process measurable, document decisions, reduce recurring failures and preserve repeatability when responsibility changes hands.

My quality-management work includes process mapping, SOPs, controlled documentation and KPI tracking. My training includes issuer-specific Lean Six Sigma Green, Yellow and White Belt programmes (AIGPE, 2025), ISO 9001:2015 QMS training (IBS, 2021), and further development in laboratory and environmental management standards.

Design the process

Map the work, clarify hand-offs and identify where preventable variation enters.

Check the result

Choose useful measures, distinguish drift from random change and document decisions.

Maintain the standard

Use SOPs, traceable records and corrective-action reasoning to preserve useful improvements.

These are individual training and practice records, not claims of institutional ISO accreditation, auditor licensure or ASQ Certified Six Sigma Green Belt status.
