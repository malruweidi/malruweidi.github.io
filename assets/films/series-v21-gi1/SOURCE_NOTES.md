### Authoritative chapter

Al-Ruweidi, Mahmoud Khatib A. A.; Khater, Nada; Alkaabi, Haya Rashid; Hasan, Maram; Yalcin, Huseyin C. **Autoimmune Diseases of the GI Tract Part I: Etiology and Pathophysiology**. In *Immunology of the GI Tract – Recent Advances*, IntechOpen, **2022**, published 17 November 2022. https://doi.org/10.5772/intechopen.106184; publisher full text https://www.intechopen.com/chapters/83241 . This is a **narrative review**, not newly collected primary evidence, and it does not provide the original patient gut-biopsy footage appearing in this film.

The chapter reviews innate and adaptive immunity, epithelial barrier dysfunction, antigenic triggers, the continuum of autoimmune and autoinflammatory mechanisms, five broad GI disease groupings, microbiome dynamics and the gut–brain axis. These are scholarly frameworks with heterogeneity and causal uncertainties; the animated pathways do not resolve causation or validate clinical diagnostics.

### Diseases covered — essential intellectual distinctions

- **Achalasia:** loss of inhibitory myenteric neurons produces esophageal motility impairment. An autoimmune contribution is proposed in some contexts but not universally established.
- **Autoimmune atrophic gastritis:** immune-mediated destruction of gastric parietal cells can result in hypochlorhydria and impaired vitamin B12 absorption. **B12 deficiency is a consequence**, not the primary etiologic definition.
- **Celiac disease:** gluten-associated, HLA-linked adaptive immune enteropathy and villous injury. It is a mechanistically different entity from food-triggered, type-2 inflammation in EoE.
- **Eosinophilic esophagitis (EoE):** chronic, antigen-associated, eosinophilic **type-2 immune-mediated** disease; the broad chapter classification does not justify calling it classical organ-specific autoimmunity.
- **Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD):** Crohn's and ulcerative colitis have distinct pathology and regional patterns, and feature dysregulated innate/adaptive immune responses. They are **immune-mediated disorders**, not universally classical autoantibody-caused disease.

The film avoids a false visual equation of these five conditions. The five animated mechanism cards are schematic; no diagnostic patterns are asserted on a patient slide.
