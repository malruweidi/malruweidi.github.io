# Film 05 — Historical source and visual-reconstruction ledger

## Primary textual tradition

- *Minhāj al-dukkān wa-dustūr al-aʿyān*, associated with al-Kūhīn al-ʿAṭṭār, composed in Cairo in 658 AH / 1260 CE. The 120-second narration already used in the site's established 2026 research edition has been kept intact and is not replaced with an invented quotation.
- Wellcome Collection, **MS Arabic 1002**, later copy dated **Egypt, 1904 CE**; reference image at `assets/films/source-manuscripts/wellcome-ms-arabic-1002/canvas-013.jpg`. Archive's `source-register.json` lists it as a **Public Domain Mark** holding. IIIF manifest: https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/presentation/v2/b21417283.
- Crucial boundary: the selected page's *content is unverified*. It must **never** be described as the specific syrup instructions until independently read. This warning is overlaid near the manuscript scene.
- The film's scholarly context is the user's accepted article, *Making Medicines Keep: Syrups, Substitution, and Pharmaceutical Reliability in Ayyubid–Mamluk Cairo*, accepted by *History of Pharmacy and Pharmaceuticals* on 15 October 2026. Do not imply a released issue, online publication date, DOI, medical efficacy finding or reconstruct an imaginary journal cover.

## What the moving images represent

1. **Flame and vessel:** New interpretive graphics, a metaphor of keeping a medicine after its preparation, not a documented 1260 Cairo apparatus.
2. **Manuscript witness:** Actual Wellcome scan shown intact, no new Arabic characters added and no cropping of source lines.
3. **Rose water, sugar, additional material:** Source-motivated process visualization; molecules are not rendered in accurate mass ratios or as original measurements.
4. **Heating, skimming, straining and reconcentration:** Illustrative movements, not an actual filmed medieval historical demonstration.
5. **Consistency and crystallization:** Conceptual appearance only. No fabricated viscosity reading, quantified sugar concentration or release standard.
6. **Failure and correction:** An explanatory diagram of historical corrective practice. No recipe/formulation is presented as clinically or microbiologically validated.
7. **Substitution and unavailable supply:** Decision logic, not a claim of clinical, pharmacological or chemical equivalence. The Genizah is mentioned as documentary background, not depicted with a counterfeit manuscript image.
8. **Research closure:** The author's accepted manuscript title typeset as an editorial end-credit; no fabricated journal cover.

## Craft and review

- Film deliberately uses an **ochre / wood / pale parchment** art direction, different from films 01–04.
- Computational motion: eight authored chapters, flowing fluid/syrup, stirring, liquid movement, conditional stage-reveals, moving source locator, threshold and crystallization metaphor, transfer, substitution flow and time-based 2.5D camera drift.
- Pure seekable `frame=f(t)`; every random texture point is seeded at initialization, never drawn by random calls inside frames. Film rendered at **120 seconds, 24 fps, 1920 × 1080**.
- Existing `series-v8-primary` audio/captions retained on exact original timeline; new synthesized atmospheric score is unlicensed-free.
- Screening is still subject to **human full-length audiovisual evaluation**, historical specialist manuscript-page review, accessibility, and rights/source attribution confirmation. This branch is not a release.
