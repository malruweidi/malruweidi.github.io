## Explicit conceptual clarifications (updated guidance)
- **Coeliac disease:** gluten-triggered adaptive immune response in genetically susceptible people; autoantibodies against tissue transglutaminase and characteristic small-bowel pathology. Not every antibody test is sufficient to establish a diagnosis in isolation. The film does not stage an actual biopsy.
- **Autoimmune atrophic gastritis:** parietal-cell-directed autoimmune pathology; not equated to all gastritis.
- **Eosinophilic oesophagitis (EoE):** chronic allergen-linked **type-2 immune-mediated** oesophageal disease, not classically defined by pathogenic self-reactive autoantibodies. ACG guideline: Dellon ES et al. *American Journal of Gastroenterology* 2025;120(1):31–59. DOI https://doi.org/10.14309/ajg.0000000000003194.
- **Inflammatory bowel disease:** Crohn disease and ulcerative colitis are heterogeneous **immune-mediated** disorders with host, intestinal barrier, microbiome and environmental factors. Broadly labeling both as antibody-defined autoimmunity would overstate the consensus. See Little RD et al. *eGastroenterology* 2024;2:e100006, https://egastroenterology.bmj.com/content/2/1/e100006.
- **Achalasia:** impaired oesophageal motility; immune-mediated contributions are hypothesised for some disease presentations, but the pathogenesis is heterogeneous and cannot be reduced to one validated autoimmune mechanism.
- **Mechanistic and diagnostic inference:** gross intestinal symptoms alone, a putative barrier defect, microbiome profile, illustrative immune network or one test do **not** establish disease identity. Disease-specific clinical criteria and professional assessment remain essential.

## Six continuous moving scenes and what they *do not* claim
1. **00–14 s:** Layered rotating colonization motifs over animated mucosal epithelial and immune schematic. Not a patient tissue image or microscopic recording.
2. **14–28 s:** Conceptual dietary/microbial signals crossing near a barrier, cellular movement and modulation of tolerance. Does not assert that permeability change universally precedes disease.
3. **28–42 s:** Visually distinct innate and adaptive cells with connecting kinetics to illustrate the immunological continuum. Does not imply interchangeable mechanisms or a quantitative immune score.
4. **42–56 s:** Five separately identified GI conditions with current *immune-mediated versus autoimmune* caution. This corrects an oversimplification in the 2022 review's collective terminology.
5. **56–70 s:** Clinical-symptom, schematic histological architecture and diagnostic-criterion panes converge. No real patient slide or fabricated antigen test.
6. **70–84 s:** Full review chapter identity, DOI and explicit limitation. No therapeutic efficacy, individual prognosis or diagnosis implied.
