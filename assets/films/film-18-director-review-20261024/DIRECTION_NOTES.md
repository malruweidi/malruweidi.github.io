**Core evidence constraint:** distinguish curve-fitting, physical model, PBPK/PBBM translation, and continuously updated digital twins. A fitted in-vitro release profile does not prove the model mechanism or establish in-vivo exposure. Every plotted simulated curve should identify the governing assumptions, parameter values and qualitative/nonclinical status. No invented validation datasets or spurious clinical outcomes.
