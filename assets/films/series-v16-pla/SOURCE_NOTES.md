# Film 08 — “The Surface Is Not the Material”
## Source, research claim and animation-authenticity ledger

**Primary research:** Abdulkareem, A.; Kasak, P.; Nassr, M. G.; Mahmoud, A. A.; Al-Ruweidi, M. K. A. A.; Mohamoud, K. J.; Hussein, M. K.; Popelka, A. *Surface Modification of Poly(lactic acid) Film via Cold Plasma Assisted Grafting of Fumaric and Ascorbic Acid*. **Polymers** 2021, 13(21), 3717. https://doi.org/10.3390/polym13213717 . Original article: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0**.

### Research claims that may appear on screen
- Untreated PLA water-contact angle = **70.2°**, plasma = **55.1°**, plasma-activated fumaric-acid-grafted = **49.6°**. Source: published Table 1 and unaltered Figure 1. The film does not invent measurement uncertainty.
- PLA/aluminum laminate peel resistance (reported): untreated approximately **74.4 N/m**, untreated + fumaric acid (without prior plasma) approximately **87.2 N/m**, plasma + fumaric acid = **211 N/m**. The original Figure 8 bar chart is displayed in full with correct control labels and units. The published relative peel improvement was approximately **184%** for plasma+FA versus untreated, but this figure is not necessary to dramatize and is not equated with clinical benefit.
- Surface chemistry evaluation used **XPS**, among other methods (FTIR, SEM, AFM, contact angles, profilometry). The original **Figure 4 XPS** is shown complete, including the binding energy axis and color legend. Do not substitute a schematic XPS chart for that real evidence.
- The context of the study is **PLA film for adhesion and sustainable packaging laminates**, not a clinical implant, antimicrobial scaffold, or in vivo delivery system.

### Animation versus observation
All numerically drawn polymer films, wettability droplets, plasma particle paths, surface functional groups, graft trajectories, peel vectors and force iconography are **modern explanatory animations**, not experimental recordings or microscopy. They contain no numerical claim unless separately attested in the paper. No unknown graft molecular configuration or radical mechanism is asserted as directly imaged. The untreated/modified sheets look distinct in the illustration because the film is illustrating a measured difference, not because that appearance was documented macroscopically.

### Creative direction and QC
Eight active, continually changing visual chapters at 15 seconds each; native Full HD 1920×1080 at 24 fps and 2,880 frames. A cool metal/graphite to warm reactive amber visual transition narrates the surface event. Scientifically meaningful motion: contact droplet geometry, moving interface-bound moieties, plasma activation motifs, XPS evidence reveal, original wetting graph, peel-force mechanical separation and the necessary no-plasma control.

A genuine cinematic test must extend beyond simply counting distinct frames. Independently review source figure legibility at 1080p, the original narration with the new score, device-scale playback, transition pacing, and accessibility. Technical QA verifies source integrity, full duration, source captions, reproducible seekable frames, decoded frame variation, H.264/AAC and EBU R128 loudness; it cannot itself establish audience-level artistic excellence.
