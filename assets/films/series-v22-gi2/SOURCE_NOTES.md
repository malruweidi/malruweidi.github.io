#### Keep disease mechanisms distinct

- Achalasia: a primary motility disorder with inhibitory myenteric neuronal loss; diagnostic pathways (including manometry/FLIP) and interventions are fundamentally unlike those for celiac disease or EoE.
- Autoimmune atrophic gastritis: autoantibodies to parietal cell antigens and/or intrinsic factor can inform clinical evaluation. **Antibody positivity is not itself a complete independent diagnosis**; deficient acid secretion and B12/iron issues depend on disease circumstances.
- Celiac disease: tissue-transglutaminase serology is diagnostically relevant under appropriate total-IgA and gluten exposure assumptions, with histology and condition-specific interpretation. Strict gluten elimination is established treatment for *confirmed* celiac disease, not a universal GI diet.
- Eosinophilic esophagitis (EoE): a type-2, immune-mediated eosinophilic disorder, **not a classical autoantibody-driven autoimmune disease**. Its diagnostic standard involves context-specific esophageal findings and tissue eosinophilia, and its dietary/anti-inflammatory therapies must be individualized.
- Crohn disease and ulcerative colitis (IBD): inflammatory immune-mediated conditions with separate phenotypes and treatment pathways. Neither is interchangeable with celiac, achalasia or EoE.

Even where the original chapter uses umbrella language about GI autoimmunity, cinematic labels must not flatten these categories into the same molecular disease.

### Explanatory structure, visual truthfulness

Each of six 14-second chapters has purposeful continuous motion rather than a still-slide pan:

1. *One symptom, many causes.* Four abstract windows for immune, inflammatory, infectious and functional possibilities. These are conceptual categories, never a diagnostic differential for a specific real individual. No individual clinical observation, patient record or reconstructed sample.
2. *Choose evidence, interpret limits.* Three separate and moving illustrative views of **serology**, **endoscopic location/pattern**, and **tissue context**, labelled clearly **SIMULATED VISUALS · NOT ENDOSCOPY, BIOPSY OR LAB RESULTS**. Cartoon antibody Y-shapes are not actual antibody test results. Abstract tissue-like dots and rings are not slides or histopathology.
3. *From microscopy to algorithms.* Actual owner lab-workbench photograph from `assets/research-archive-2026/molecular-biology-pipette-workbench.webp`, labelled **NOT TRAINING DATA**, then synthetic moving scan/features/AI-model panels. Features are not a trained or validated diagnostic algorithm, output tensor or heat map. No fabricated precision, sensitivity or external-accuracy claims.
4. *Treatment follows mechanism.* Five illustrative therapy categories, mapping celiac to a gluten-free diet; EoE to condition-specific diet/anti-inflammatory choices; achalasia to motility/structural intervention; AAG to nutrient-deficiency evaluation; IBD to disease-specific therapy. They do **not** prescribe treatment, establish that any therapy is appropriate for a particular patient, or summarize full contemporary guidelines.
5. *A new tool still needs proof.* A continuously animated chain of assay and image-reading concepts to biomarker, algorithm, clinical validation and potential use. Edges are design logic, **not identified causal paths or real estimates**. Source-to-practice requires appropriate sensitivity, specificity, external validation and real clinical benefit.
6. *An evolving map, not a prescription.* Genuine owner archival biological safety cabinet photograph, clearly contextual, and a final safety gate noting the 2022 evidence vintage and need for specialist evaluation with updated guidance.
