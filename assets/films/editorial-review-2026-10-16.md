# Editorial review / Evidence in Motion
16 October 2026 · Four two-minute Ruwaidix films

## What was directly examined
Selected rendered source plates from each of the four movies, including the historical pharmacy opening, prescription, process, and end credit; pulmonary interface and published flow-chamber figure; actual failed zebrafish microtomy photograph; ACS and scientific-community photographs. The issue descriptions below arise from the rendered frame images, not from a speculative screenplay review.

## Visual corrections
* **Making Medicines Keep:** The previous opening and qiwām chapters repeated the same julāb bottle art; new specific process compositions differentiate material deterioration, julāb as base, consistency as process endpoint, and sugar crystallization.
* **Readability:** Remove decorative circles overlapping the historical syrup text; reposition the Genizah's explanatory caption; shorten long film header labels without chopping words; preserve margins between the illustrated source and title.
* **Scientific figures:** Retain full crop and original aspect ratio in the film, and give visitors a one-click way to open the underlying figure at larger resolution. No claim that a conceptual diagram is a micrograph.
* **Mobile captions:** Switch from two overly long cues per scene to several 64-character-at-most units, still timed proportionally across source narration. These are approximate word timings, not forced alignment.

## Technical audio quality (automated, not auditory judgment)
The separate `evidence-series-technical-qa.json` records exact runtime, codec, image size, frame rate, EBU R128 integrated loudness, true peak, caption duration and longest cue for each 120-second file. It also verifies that the historical illustrations actually differ within the main graphic area.

## What still requires human listening and viewing
A technical loudness check cannot tell whether a voice sounds emotionally convincing or whether the mix is pleasing; subtitle proportional timing should be visually reviewed in playback. In particular, listen to `Minhāj al-dukkān`, `julāb`, `qiwām`, `Ayyubid-Mamluk`, and `L2` while viewing the film. Pronunciation has not been certified or human-auditioned during this automated review, and we should not claim otherwise. A complete continuous two-minute screening on mobile and desktop is also still an editorial task.

## Historical evidence boundary
The article was accepted 15 October 2026, not here represented as having a final published DOI. The Genizah original Or.1081 J39 scan is linked for context, not downloaded or republished without permission. Historical scenes remain visibly editorial. The failed histology image remains evidence of an unsuccessful attempted procedure and the film does not turn it into a successful result.
